Invisible No More

Living with a disability can be challenging, but it doesn't have to be that way. Here at Project Unveiling Scars, we can make your voices heard.

Our Mission

At Project Unveiling Scars, we advocate for the millions living with invisible disabilities. By partnering with leading organizations and hosting events, Project Unveiling Scars ensures every individual is seen, heard, and supported. Everyone deserves to have their stories told, and those with disabilities are no different.
