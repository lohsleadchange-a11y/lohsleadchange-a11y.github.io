Our Team

Meet the people working on Project Unveiling Scars.

Committee Lead

Jennica Alexis

"Hi! My name is Jennica Alixes and I am the Committee Chair for this year's Lead4Change project. After spending a very successful year with our project last year, I hope to lead our team to making a bigger impact and difference for individuals with invisible disabilities all across the United States with a close knit bond from our partner, Easterseals. Our project has brought me absolute joy with the visible changes and influences that are seen across not only our local community in Rancho Cucamonga, but also across Southern California and the communities that we have brought together for a cause that resonates with all. From elementary kids to seniors, we have taken the time, dedication, and courage to become the voice for many that have stayed hidden in the shadows of stigma or lack of awareness. By leading this wonderful team, I hope that our difference will make the next generation be more educated and aware of how broad invisible disabilities are."
