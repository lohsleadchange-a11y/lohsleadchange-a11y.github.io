About Disabilities

Tens of millions of people live with disabilities. While many are easily noticeable, the majority are invisible and are thus difficult to recognize. Project Unveiling Scars adovocates for individuals with both invisible and visible disabilties, amplifying their concerns and guiding them through challenges.

Lifting Burdens, Changing Lives

We provide a vital safety net through financial aid for families in need, ensuring those with disabilities can lead normal lives, while acting as a strong advocate for those navigating the complexities of invisible disabilities.

Amplifying the Unseen

We cultivate safe, high-profile platforms where lived experiences take center stage. By creating spaces for storytelling, we turn private challenges into a powerful public movement, ensuring that every individual is seen, heard, and valued.

Scaling Impact Together

Real change doesn’t happen in isolation. We collaborate with industry-leading organizations, such as Easterseals, to dismantle stigma and drive large-scale social shifts. Together, we are building a world designed for everyone.
